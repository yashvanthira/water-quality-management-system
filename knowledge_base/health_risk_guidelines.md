# Public Health Risks, Contaminants, and Citizen Safety Advisories

## 1. Microbiological Contamination (Coliforms and Fecal Streptococci)
- **Source**: Untreated domestic sewage discharge, open defecation along riverbanks, animal waste runoff, and overflowing storm drains.
- **Indicators**: Total Coliform (MPN/100ml) and Fecal Coliform (MPN/100ml).
- **Health Dangers**:
  - **Waterborne Diseases**: Acute bacterial diarrhea, cholera (*Vibrio cholerae*), typhoid fever (*Salmonella typhi*), dysentery (*Shigella*), and viral hepatitis A & E.
  - **Skin & Eye Infections**: Bathing in water exceeding 500-2500 MPN/100ml can cause conjunctivitis, ear canal infections, and dermatitis.
  - **High-Risk Groups**: Infants, elderly individuals, pregnant women, and immuno-compromised patients.

## 2. Organic Pollution (Biochemical Oxygen Demand - BOD)
- **What BOD Measures**: The amount of dissolved oxygen required by aerobic biological organisms to break down organic material in a given water sample at a specific temperature.
- **Normal Range**: Pristine waters have BOD < 1 mg/L. Moderately clean rivers have BOD 1-3 mg/L.
- **High BOD (> 3 to 30+ mg/L)**:
  - Indicates massive organic dumping (sewage, food processing, dairy, slaughterhouses, paper mills).
  - Rapidly depletes Dissolved Oxygen (DO), causing fish kills, anoxic black water, and foul sulfurous odor (hydrogen sulfide).
  - Bathing in high-BOD water exposes citizens to dangerous pathogenic bacteria feeding on the organic matter.

## 3. Dissolved Oxygen (DO) Depletion
- **Critical Threshold**:
  - $\ge 6$ mg/L: Healthy river ecosystem, supports sensitive game fish (Mahseer, Trout).
  - $4$ to $5$ mg/L: Stressed aquatic fauna, coarse fish survival only.
  - $< 3$ mg/L: Hypoxic dead zone; asphyxiation of aquatic life.
  - $< 1$ mg/L: Septic condition; anaerobic bacteria produce methane and hydrogen sulfide gases.

## 4. Nitrate Nitrogen Pollution
- **Source**: Agricultural fertilizer runoff (urea, NPK), animal feedlots, and sewage leakage.
- **Threshold**: $> 10$ mg/L (as Nitrate-N) or $> 45$ mg/L (as $\text{NO}_3$).
- **Health Impact**:
  - **Methemoglobinemia**: Nitrates convert hemoglobin to methemoglobin in infants, which cannot carry oxygen, turning skin blue and causing suffocation.
  - **Chronic Toxicity**: Linked to thyroid hypertrophy and increased gastrointestinal cancer risks.

## 5. Public Safety Decision Tree for Citizens
- **Is the water safe for Drinking?**
  - Only if treated through approved BIS 10500 municipal supply or certified multi-stage RO/UV filtration.
  - Direct consumption from rivers, canals, ponds, or drains is STRICTLY PROHIBITED.
- **Is the water safe for Bathing / Dipping?**
  - SAFE if: DO $\ge 5$ mg/L, BOD $\le 3$ mg/L, Total Coliform $\le 500$ MPN/100ml.
  - CAUTION if: BOD is between 3-5 mg/L or Coliform is between 500-2,500 MPN/100ml. Avoid submerging head, rinsing mouth, or entering with open wounds.
  - DANGEROUS/PROHIBITED if: BOD $> 5$ mg/L or Coliform $> 2,500$ MPN/100ml.
- **Is it safe for cattle / livestock?**
  - Cattle should not drink from industrial drains, stagnant ponds with algal blooms, or water with Total Coliform $> 5,000$ MPN/100ml.
