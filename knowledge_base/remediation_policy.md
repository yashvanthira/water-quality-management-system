# Water Governance, Remediation Guidelines & Citizen Grievance Redressal

## 1. Statutory Framework in India
- **The Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974**:
  - Establishes the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and State Pollution Control Boards (SPCBs).
  - Section 24: Prohibits the disposal of polluting matter into streams, wells, or land.
  - Section 25: Mandates consent from SPCB before establishing industrial discharges or outlets.
  - Section 33A: Empowers Boards to issue closure directions and stop electricity/water supply to polluting units.
- **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986**:
  - Sets national discharge standards for Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) and Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETPs).
- **National Green Tribunal (NGT) Directives**:
  - Mandates rejuvenation of polluted river stretches across India.
  - Stipulates strict environmental compensation penalties for urban local bodies releasing untreated sewage.

## 2. Engineering Remediation & Interventions
- **In-Situ Bioremediation**: Using non-pathogenic bacterial consortia and floating bio-islands to digest organic matter in open nallahs.
- **Interception & Diversion (I&D)**: Trapping untreated sewage drains before they reach main river courses and diverting them to decentralized STPs.
- **Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs)**:
  - Strict CPCB revised discharge norms: BOD $\le 10$ mg/L, TSS $\le 20$ mg/L, COD $\le 50$ mg/L, Total Nitrogen $\le 10$ mg/L, Fecal Coliform $< 230$ MPN/100ml.
- **Riparian Buffer Zones**: Planting native bamboo, vetiver grass, and reeds along riverbanks to filter agricultural runoff and stabilize banks.

## 3. Citizen Grievance & Reporting Protocol
When water monitoring reveals hazardous contamination (e.g., BOD $> 5$ mg/L, high coliform, illegal industrial outfall):
1. **Document the Evidence**: Record Station Code, Location Name, Water Body Type, Date/Year, and measured parameter exceedances.
2. **Key Authorities to Address**:
   - Respective State Pollution Control Board (SPCB) / Pollution Control Committee (PCC).
   - District Magistrate (DM) / Collector (Chairperson of District Environment Committee).
   - Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) Parivesh Portal / Sameer App.
   - National Green Tribunal (NGT) Principal Bench or Zonal Bench for public interest environmental petitions.
3. **Draft Complaint Format**:
   - Subject: Urgent Notice of Water Contamination & Violation of Water Act 1974 at [Location Name].
   - Cite baseline metrics vs actual monitored values.
   - Request immediate water sample re-testing, drone survey of industrial outfalls, and commissioning of sewage treatment infrastructure.
