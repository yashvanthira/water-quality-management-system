# Scientific Water Purification and Treatment Guidelines (BIS 10500 & WHO Protocols)

## 1. Contaminant-Specific Purification Matrix

| Detected Contaminant | Ineffective / Dangerous Methods | Mandatory Effective Technologies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Microbial Pathogens (Coliforms, Fecal Bacteria, E. coli)** | Simple cloth filtration, sedimentation | Boiling (vigorous roll $\ge 1$ min), Chlorination ($2-5\text{ mg/L}$ free chlorine), UV irradiation, Microfiltration ($< 0.2\text{ }\mu\text{m}$). |
| **Nitrate-Nitrogen ($\text{NO}_3\text{-N} > 10\text{ mg/L}$)** | **DANGER: Boiling** (evaporates water, concentrating nitrate ions, severely elevating methemoglobinemia risk in infants) | Reverse Osmosis (RO), Strong Base Anion (SBA) Ion-Exchange Resins, Electrodialysis Reversal (EDR). |
| **High Organic Load / High BOD ($> 3\text{ mg/L}$)** | Simple boiling, chlorination alone (forms hazardous trihalomethanes - THMs) | Coagulation-flocculation, Granular Activated Carbon (GAC) adsorption, Aeration, Ozonation. |
| **High Total Dissolved Solids / Conductivity ($> 1500\text{ }\mu\text{mho/cm}$)** | Boiling, UV, standard filters | Reverse Osmosis (RO) with semi-permeable membrane ($0.0001\text{ }\mu\text{m}$), Distillation. |
| **Turbidity / Suspended Solids** | Direct disinfection without pre-treatment | Alum coagulation, Multi-grade sand filtration, Dual-media depth filtration. |
| **Low Dissolved Oxygen ($< 4\text{ mg/L}$)** | Passive storage | Mechanical aeration, stepped cascade aerators, venture oxygen injection. |

## 2. Multi-Barrier Household Potable Water Protocol
For raw surface water (Rivers, Lakes, Canals, Wells) to reach potable safety:
1. **Stage 1 - Clarification & Pre-Sedimentation**: Allow suspended solids to settle or filter through a 5-micron sediment cartridge.
2. **Stage 2 - Organic Removal**: Pass through Granular Activated Carbon (GAC) to remove dissolved organics, pesticide traces, and chlorine-reactive organic matter.
3. **Stage 3 - Demineralization / Heavy Contaminant Removal**: Pass through Reverse Osmosis (RO) if conductivity $\ge 1000\text{ }\mu\text{mho/cm}$ or nitrate $\ge 10\text{ mg/L}$.
4. **Stage 4 - Microbial Disinfection**: UV chamber exposure or vigorous boiling for at least 1 minute (3 minutes at high altitude) immediately prior to consumption.

## 3. Community & Municipal Ecological Remediation
1. **In-Situ Bio-Remediation**: Inoculation of nitrifying and aerobic bacterial consortia in polluted river stretches or nallahs to digest organic sludge.
2. **Constructed Floating Treatment Wetlands (FTWs)**: Deploying floating mats of native plants (Vetiver, Typha, Phragmites) whose roots absorb nitrates, phosphates, and heavy metals.
3. **Decentralized Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs)**: Sequential Batch Reactors (SBR) or Membrane Bioreactors (MBR) achieving BOD $< 10\text{ mg/L}$ and Fecal Coliform $< 100\text{ MPN/100ml}$.
