# Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) Water Quality Criteria & Standards

## 1. Designated Best Use Classification System

The Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) of India has classified inland surface waters into five distinct categories based on their primary designated use:

### Class A: Drinking Water Source Without Conventional Treatment but After Disinfection
- **Total Coliform Organism**: Maximum 50 MPN/100ml
- **pH Range**: 6.5 to 8.5
- **Dissolved Oxygen (DO)**: Minimum 6.0 mg/L
- **Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD 5-day at 20°C)**: Maximum 2.0 mg/L
- **Suitability**: Pristine water sources, mountain streams, upland reservoirs. Safe for human consumption after standard chlorination/disinfection.

### Class B: Outdoor Bathing (Organized)
- **Total Coliform Organism**: Maximum 500 MPN/100ml (Desirable: 500, Max Permissible: 2500 MPN/100ml)
- **Fecal Coliform Organism**: Maximum 500 MPN/100ml
- **pH Range**: 6.5 to 8.5
- **Dissolved Oxygen (DO)**: Minimum 5.0 mg/L
- **Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD 5-day at 20°C)**: Maximum 3.0 mg/L
- **Suitability**: Holy bathing ghats, recreational swimming, religious dipping during festivals (e.g., Kumbh Mela, Chhath Puja). Safe for direct skin contact.

### Class C: Drinking Water Source After Conventional Treatment and Disinfection
- **Total Coliform Organism**: Maximum 5,000 MPN/100ml
- **pH Range**: 6.0 to 9.0
- **Dissolved Oxygen (DO)**: Minimum 4.0 mg/L
- **Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD 5-day at 20°C)**: Maximum 3.0 mg/L
- **Suitability**: Municipal raw water intake for water treatment plants (WTPs) utilizing coagulation, sedimentation, filtration, and disinfection.

### Class D: Propagation of Wildlife and Fisheries
- **pH Range**: 6.5 to 8.5
- **Dissolved Oxygen (DO)**: Minimum 4.0 mg/L
- **Free Ammonia (as N)**: Maximum 1.2 mg/L
- **Suitability**: Supports freshwater ecosystems, commercial fisheries, sanctuaries, and aquatic biodiversity. Not recommended for direct human consumption or bathing.

### Class E: Irrigation, Industrial Cooling, and Controlled Waste Disposal
- **pH Range**: 6.0 to 8.5
- **Electrical Conductivity (EC)**: Maximum 2,250 µmho/cm
- **Sodium Absorption Ratio (SAR)**: Maximum 26
- **Boron**: Maximum 2.0 mg/L
- **Suitability**: Agricultural watering of crops, non-contact industrial cooling, and controlled effluent discharge channels.

### Below Class E / Non-Compliant / Severely Polluted
- Any water body with:
  - BOD > 3 to 5 mg/L
  - Dissolved Oxygen < 4.0 mg/L (or near 0 mg/L in septic drains)
  - Total Coliform > 5,000 MPN/100ml (often ranging into hundreds of thousands or millions in open nallahs)
- **Suitability**: Unfit for any human contact, drinking, bathing, or animal consumption. Requires urgent interceptor sewers, effluent treatment, and biological aeration.
